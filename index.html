What it does

One device that measures capillary glucose and records each insulin dose, so glucose, dose and software version are stored together for the same person.

Data it creates

Glucose reading, dose units and time, and the firmware and app version for every record.

Link to our review

Our review found that regulators rarely link software version, patient use and clinical outcomes for the same patients over time. This concept is designed to create that linked record from day one.

Example screen values are illustrative only. This concept gives no dosing advice; dose decisions stay with the person and their clinician.

FROM REVIEW TO DESIGN

Why a pen-meter?

Our six-regulator review proposed following three things together after authorisation: software version, patient engagement and clinical outcomes. A connected pen-meter is one way to capture all three in a single record, for people with type 2 diabetes who use insulin.

1 · MeasureTest strip in the meter end gives a capillary glucose reading.
2 · DoseThe dial sensor records units and time of each injection.
3 · TagEvery record carries the firmware and app version.
4 · SyncBluetooth to the app, then to the care team. A missing sync is a data gap, not proof of non-use.
5 · ReviewClinic and post-market review of version, use and outcomes together.

Which build approach?

Authors’ proposed pathway, for discussion. Device classification and combination-product rules differ by jurisdiction and must be confirmed with each regulator.

OPTION A

Integrated pen-meter

  • One device to carry; glucose and dose captured together
  • Hardest to build; tied to specific insulin cartridges
  • Heaviest regulatory and manufacturing path
OPTION B · RECOMMENDED FIRST STEP

Modular: smart pen attachment + connected meter or CGM

  • Works with existing pens and meters
  • Fastest way to test the linked data model in a pilot
  • Learn what matters before building Option A
OPTION C

App only, manual entry

  • Cheapest and quickest
  • Depends on people typing data in
  • Weakest link between dose, glucose and version
